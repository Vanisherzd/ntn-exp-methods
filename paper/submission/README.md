## Build invariants, enforced mechanically

`make -C paper verify` fails unless the log shows six pages, zero LaTeX errors, zero
undefined references, zero undefined citations and zero overfull boxes. The PDF target
additionally has `scripts/check_banlist.py` as a hard prerequisite, so a build cannot
succeed while a prohibited claim is present in any source file.

## Allowed claims

- Chronological splitting constrains ordering and does not by itself establish
  availability, row membership, or the contents of a learner's hidden state.
- On our fault matrix, chronological checks detect 2 of 18 fault classes; the contract
  detects 18 of 18 across three deterministic environments (54 of 54 cells).
- Four mutations were specified before their detectors were written; two of those had no
  predecessor detector of any kind. All four were detected.
- Zero clean-path false positives in the final fixture; clean verdicts identical across
  environments.
- Two case-study pipelines were halted by a contract rule before any conclusion was
  drawn, and in the second case the pre-registered blind evaluation seeds were never
  executed.
- The toolkit is 812 lines across four modules plus a 399-line two-sided test suite,
  with `numpy` as the only dependency.

## Prohibited claims

Enforced by `paper/scripts/check_banlist.py`; source of truth
`archive/KNOWN_INVALID_RESULTS.md`.

- Any performance figure from the retired residual-Doppler line.
- Any statement that residual Doppler learning, or an evidence gate, improves or
  degrades real link, packet or LR-FHSS performance.
- Any endpoint budget, guard-time or energy conclusion.
- Any claim that the contract is complete, or that it invalidates published work.
