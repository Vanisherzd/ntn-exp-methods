\caption{\rulename{L4.7}'s operating characteristic---the only component of the contract whose
error rates are measured rather than assumed. Specificity: \num{19} false halts over \num{450}
clean replicates, $0.042$ against nominal $0.05$ (red, Wilson interval). Power: $1.00$ at
intraclass correlation $0.8$, $0.28$ at $0.2$, and indistinguishable from the false-halt rate below
about $0.1$ (shaded). A gate needs both halves; reporting specificity alone would hide that the
rule finds gross unit errors and misses subtle ones. Below four coarser groups or eight units it returns
\textsc{indeterminate}, not \textsc{pass}.}
